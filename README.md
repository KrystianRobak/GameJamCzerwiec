# *Ja i moje Zwłoki*

## Overview

This project was created as part of a game jam. It’s a first-person puzzle adventure focusing on dimension-switching mechanics and environmental storytelling.

![obraz](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b8f80e11-9dc8-43dd-877c-2ab82fc52261)

## Gameplay

You play as a character who has just died under mysterious circumstances. By interacting with your own body, you can switch between two parallel realms:

### Living Realm
- Freely explore the environment and walk through doors.
- Inspect 3D objects by picking them up and rotating them.
- Useful for uncovering hidden clues and story elements.

![obraz](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9e2d5e01-5449-4fce-8dde-5722c3089a7d)


### Spirit Realm
- Physics-based interaction: pick up and manipulate objects.
- Required for solving environmental puzzles.
- Collision is enabled — you can’t walk through objects.

![obraz](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e85a415d-1d7c-4439-813c-3bb7cb9301f1)

### Goal
Solve puzzles, uncover the mystery behind your death, and perform a ritual to free your spirit.

## Features
-  **Dual-realm switching** (Living and Spirit)
-  **Physics-driven puzzle solving** in the Spirit Realm
-  **3D object inspection** for environmental storytelling
-  **Narrative-driven gameplay** with ritual-based progression
